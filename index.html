How it works

What the code contains, which phones read it, and how to keep your network safe.

A text format phones already read

Phones understand a small text format for Wi-Fi details: the network name, the type of security and the password. This page writes your details in that format and draws it as a QR code. When someone scans it with their camera, the phone offers to join the network – no typing a long password letter by letter.

Which phones can scan it?

Any recent iPhone (iOS 11 and later) and most Android phones (Android 10 and later) read Wi-Fi codes straight from the built-in camera. Older Android phones can use Google Lens or a scanner app.

Is the shape safe to use?

Yes. The codes use the highest level of error correction, which leaves room to trim the corners into a circle, hexagon or triangle. Keep the colour dark against the white card and print the code at least 3 cm wide.

A note on security

Anyone who scans the card can join your network, so treat it like the password itself. For visitors, consider a separate guest network, and print a new card whenever you change the password.
